Accessibility

Motorhome Tools aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What we aim for

We want every page on motorhometools.co.uk to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. Pages are in British English. Text can be resized. Links and buttons are named. Calculator totals sit in a live region so a change can be heard.

Known issues

  • On a short landscape phone, the Power and Water calculators show “Turn your phone upright” and the form cannot be used until the phone is portrait. That keeps the controls large enough to tap. It does block landscape use.
  • This statement is our own check. We have not commissioned an external WCAG audit.
  • The contact form can be sent without JavaScript. The styled success and error text on the form itself needs JavaScript. Without it, the next page says whether the message was received.

Report a problem

If something is hard to use, tell Motorhome Tools through the contact form. Say the page, what you were trying to do, and the browser or screen reader if you know it. A person reads the message. A reply is not automatic.
