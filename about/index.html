About Motorhome Tools

Free guidance calculators for payload, power and water. Planning estimates you can check.

We don’t invent tyre pressures, weights or legal advice.

Who built this

Wayne Robinson

Wayne Robinson built Motorhome Tools. He is the author named on these pages.

Unmatched questions from Ask reach the site owner. Replies are not automated. We never email a tyre pressure, a weight or legal advice.

Why this exists

Plain UK calculators for planning a trip. You enter your own figures and the page estimates from those. We do not invent safety numbers. The plate, the handbook and a weighbridge ticket still win.

What the tools will do

  • Payload lives on motorhomepayload.co.uk. Weigh the van, then see a planning estimate of what you can still add. You can start from a UK registration on that page.
  • Power and Water live on this site: daily power, battery and solar; tanks, gas and the cassette. Figures stay in the browser. No account.
  • Guides are short and in plain English, then they point at the matching calculator.

The calculators are free guidance tools. They help you see what to check. They are not a weighbridge ticket, a tyre sticker or a legal document.

What they will not do

  • We don’t invent tyre pressures, weights or legal advice.
  • Tyres is limited. It is not the main path, and we don’t invent pressures. Open it only if your wheels differ from the originals.
  • The route planner is not live yet.
  • This is not legal, safety or professional advice. If you are unsure, weigh the van, read the plate, and ask a fitter or the maker.
