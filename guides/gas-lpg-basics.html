Motorhome gas / LPG bottles and safety

Motorhome gas / LPG in plain English is a map of leisure gas: cookers, fridge (if gas), heating, and bottles — not a pressure or safety certificate, and not invented burn times.

What this is for

Knowing what your van is built for before you swap a bottle or guess how long it will last. Full bottles are also weight — see fresh and waste tanks for the same payload idea.

Bottle types

UK vans often use Campingaz or BBQ-style cylinders, or fixed tanks. Read what your van is built for. Do not mix incompatible regulators or bottles.

Changeover is in the appliance and bottle maker instructions — we will not invent a jet size, a pressure, or a “typical” burn time.

Winter note

Gas performance and frost on bottles can change in cold weather — follow your heater and bottle maker guidance. We don’t invent frost rules.

If you are unsure about the installation, check a gas-safe fitter. This page is not a certificate.

Sources

Appliance and bottle maker instructions, plus a gas-safe installer where the pipework or regulator is in doubt. Never invent jet sizes or pressures.
