How you may use this site. Planning estimates only.

Using this site

Motorhome Tools publishes free calculators and guides at motorhometools.co.uk. You do not need an account. You may browse the pages, use the calculators for your own planning, and share links to them.

Planning estimates only

The figures are planning estimates. They help you see what to check. They are not a weighbridge ticket, a tyre sticker or a legal document. We don’t invent tyre pressures, weights or legal advice. The vehicle maker, the VIN plate on the van, and a qualified fitter still win. See the Disclaimer.

Your figures

You type your own numbers. Power and Water keep those figures in the browser on this site. Payload and Tyres keep figures in the browser on motorhomepayload.co.uk. We do not upload your van from this page.

Ask notes

If you type a question we do not cover, the note is for the site owner. Replies are not automated. Optional email is stored only if you type one. See Privacy.

Fair use

Do not misuse the site, try to break it, or use it in a way that stops other people using it. Do not present a calculator result as an official weight, pressure or legal ruling.

Changes

We may change these pages and the calculators. The page you are reading is the one that applies. If you keep using the site after a change, you are using it on these terms.
